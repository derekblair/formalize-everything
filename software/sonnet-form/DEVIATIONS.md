Status meanings:

- `scholarly_pair_support`: Same pair is explicitly identified in a scholarly historical discussion; supports rhyme feasibility, not a unique phonetic reconstruction.
- `scholarly_rule_inference`: Author inference from a documented pair/rule to a different word, inflection, or source-number/form discrepancy; remains open for exact pair attestation.
- `partial_support`: Evidence addresses only part of the modern difference; unresolved remainder is explicit.
- `modern_variant_only`: A documented modern alternative is a diagnostic, not historical attestation.
- `open`: Historical relation not established by the examined sources.
- `same_token_obstruction`: Distinctness impossible for an identical normalized token under deterministic context-free lookup, irrespective of historical pronunciation.
- `scholarly_repeated_class`: Scholarly observation of repeated rhyme family; rejects strict class-distinctness without implying defective verse.
- `modern_collision_open`: Observed modern equality under distinct signature letters; historical identity or separation remains open.
- `source_structure`: Corroborated non-fourteen-line source structure; no fourteen-position pair comparison is imposed.
- `scholarly_imperfect_analysis`: The cited scholar explicitly treats the pair as imperfect rhyme, assonance, or unequal sounds. This supports a nonexact analysis; it does not rule out every other historical reconstruction.
- `scholarly_disagreement`: Consulted scholars give different exactness analyses for the same pair. The historical relation remains open pending reconciliation or independent evidence.
