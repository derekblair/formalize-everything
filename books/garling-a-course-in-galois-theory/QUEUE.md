## Ch. 1, Exercise 1.18 (§1.3, printed p. 13) — false printed lower bound for `r ≥ 3` and vacuous `r ≤ 1` cover (fidelity hold)

- **Printed statement:** *"Suppose that $V$ is a vector space of dimension $r$ over a finite field $K$ with $k$ elements. Show that if $V = \bigcup_{i=1}^n U_i$, where $U_1, \dots, U_n$ are proper linear subspaces of $V$, then $n \ge (k^r - 1) / (k - 1)$, and show that there exist $(k^r - 1) / (k - 1)$ proper linear subspaces of $V$ whose union is $V$."*
- **Exact blockers:**
  1. **First claim is mathematically false as printed for every $r \ge 3$:** Garling's counting bound $(k^r - 1)/(k - 1)$ counts $1$-dimensional subspaces (each containing $k - 1$ non-zero vectors), whereas a proper subspace of an $r$-dimensional space can have dimension $r - 1$ and contain $k^{r-1} - 1$ non-zero vectors. Over $K = \mathbb{F}_2$ ($k = 2$) and $V = K^3$ ($r = 3$), $(k^r - 1)/(k - 1) = 7$, yet $V$ is covered by $n = 3 < 7$ proper subspaces (the three $2$-dimensional hyperplanes $v_2 = 0$, $v_3 = 0$, and $v_2 + v_3 = 0$). More generally, pulling back the $k + 1$ lines of $K^2$ along a surjective linear map $V \twoheadrightarrow K^2$ covers any $V$ of dimension $r \ge 2$ with $n = k + 1$ proper hyperplanes, and $k + 1 < (k^r - 1)/(k - 1)$ whenever $r \ge 3$.
  2. **Second claim requires $r \ge 2$:** When $r \le 1$ ($r = 0$ or $r = 1$), every proper subspace of $V$ has dimension $< r$ (no proper subspaces when $r = 0$, only $\{0\}$ when $r = 1$), so **no** finite union of proper subspaces equals $V$.
- **Kernel-proved declarations in `Garling/Chapter01.lean`:**
  - `Garling.Chapter01.exercise_1_18_printed_lower_bound_counterexample`: exhibits `U : Fin 3 → Submodule (ZMod 2) (Kn 3 (ZMod 2))` with `setOrder (ZMod 2) = 2`, `garlingDim = 3`, `∀ i, U i ≠ ⊤`, `⋃ i, U i = Set.univ`, and `3 < (2^3 - 1) / (2 - 1)`.
  - `Garling.Chapter01.exercise_1_18_no_proper_cover_of_dim_le_one`: proves that if `garlingDim K V hV ≤ 1`, no finite family of proper subspaces covers `V`.
  - `Garling.Chapter01.exercise_1_18_sharp_lower_bound`: proves the true sharp lower bound `k + 1 ≤ n` for any cover of an `r`-dimensional space over a `k`-element field by `n` arbitrary proper subspaces.
  - `Garling.Chapter01.exercise_1_18_one_dim_lower_bound`: proves the printed bound `(k^r - 1) / (k - 1) ≤ n` under the hypothesis `∀ i, dim U_i ≤ 1` (which includes all proper subspaces when `r = 2`).
  - `Garling.Chapter01.exercise_1_18_exists_proper_subspaces_union`: proves that for `2 ≤ r`, there exist `(k^r - 1) / (k - 1)` pairwise distinct proper subspaces of `V` whose union is `V`.
