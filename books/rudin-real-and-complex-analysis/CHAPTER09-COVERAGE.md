## Representation and fidelity disclosures

1. **Rudin's normalized measure and Fourier kernel (`9.1`–`9.23`):** Every Chapter 9 statement is formalized in Rudin's exact normalization `dm(x) = dx / √(2π)` (`rudinMeasure := (rudinNNFactor : ℝ≥0∞) • volume`) with forward kernel `exp(-i x t)` (`rudinFourier`), inverse kernel `exp(i x t)` (`rudinFourierInv`), `L^p(m)` spaces `RudinLp p := Lp ℂ p rudinMeasure`, and convolution `(f * g)(x) = ∫ f(x - y) g(y) dm(y)` (`rudinConv`). Both adapter bridges to Mathlib's `exp(-2π i x t) dx` Fourier transform `𝓕` are proved explicitly: the direct scaling identities `rudinFourier_eq_mathlib_fourier` / `rudinFourierInv_eq_mathlib_fourierInv` (`L321–L340`) and the isometric coordinate-dilation equivalence `rudinScaleLpEquiv : RudinLp p ≃ₗᵢ[ℂ] Lp ℂ p volume` intertwining `rudinDilate` with `𝓕`, `𝓕⁻`, and `MeasureTheory.convolution` (`L344–L439`).
2. **Arbitrary-`L¹` inversion without continuity (`9.4`, `9.11`, `9.14`):** Neither `theorem_9_4_circle_l1_inversion` (on `CircleL1`) nor `theorem_9_11` (on `Integrable f rudinMeasure`) assumes continuity of `f`; both recover `f` almost everywhere from the continuous inverse series/integral. Similarly, `theorem_9_14` requires only `f ∈ L²(m)` (`f : RudinLp 2`) and `Integrable (rudinPlancherel f) rudinMeasure`, without assuming `f ∈ L¹(m)`.
3. **Essential boundedness in `9.9`:** `theorem_9_9` takes `MemLp g ∞ rudinMeasure` and `ContinuousAt g x`, matching the printed `g ∈ L^∞` hypothesis without strengthening to global pointwise boundedness.
4. **Nonunital Banach algebras and automatic continuity (`9.18`–`9.21`):** `IsRudinBanachAlgebra` (`9.18`) does not require a multiplicative identity, and `RudinComplexHom A := A →ₙₐ[ℂ] ℂ` (`9.20`) is purely algebraic (including `0`, proved in `theorem_9_20_zero_mem`, with no continuity or boundedness field). Automatic continuity and `‖h‖ ≤ 1` are proved unconditionally at `9.21` (`theorem_9_21_apply_le`, `rudinComplexHomToCLM`, `theorem_9_21`) via the `L¹`-norm unitization `WithLp 1 (Unitization ℂ A)`.
