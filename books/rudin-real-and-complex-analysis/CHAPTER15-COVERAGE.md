## Representation and fidelity disclosures

1. **Zero-allowing sequential infinite products (`15.2`–`15.5`):** Per [SCOPING-G.md](SCOPING-G.md) Caution 2, Rudin's infinite-product definition counts a zero limit of finite initial partial products as convergent, unlike Mathlib's unconditional `HasProd`/`tprod`. `HasSequentialProduct`, `ProductConverges`, `HasInfiniteProductOneAdd`, and `InfiniteProductOneAddConverges` retain the exact sequential partial-product limit (`0` allowed), and `theorem15_4` retains boundedness of each `u_n` on an arbitrary set `S` together with uniform convergence of `∑ ‖u_n(s)‖` on `S`.
2. **Proper open subsets of the Riemann sphere (`15.11`):** Per [SCOPING-G.md](SCOPING-G.md) Caution 3, `theorem15_11` (`L2486`) works directly on any proper open subset `Ω ≠ Set.univ` of `RiemannSphere = OnePoint ℂ` (including neighborhoods of `∞` via Möbius chart transport `z ↦ (z - p₀)⁻¹` from an omitted point `p₀ ∈ Ωᶜ`), defining `HolomorphicOnSphere` and `sphereZeroOrder` across both finite points and `∞`.
3. **Meromorphic quotients (`15.12`) and disconnected/zero-generator Bézout ideals (`15.14`–`15.15`):** `theorem15_12` (`L2901`) proves the global quotient representation `g / h` (`g, h ∈ H(Ω)`, `h` not identically zero on any component of `Ω`) for both `Chapter10.BookMeromorphicOn` and Mathlib's `MeromorphicOn`, together with its converse. `theorem15_15_concrete` (`L3761`) and `theorem15_15` (`L3817`) prove `IsBezout (HolomorphicRing Ω)` and principal generation for arbitrary open `Ω ⊆ ℂ`, explicitly handling disconnected components and identically zero generators via `15.11` and `15.13`.
4. **Circle boundary zeros (`15.18`), extended-real `log 0 = -∞` (`15.19`), and origin-zero removal (`15.20`):** Per [SCOPING-G.md](SCOPING-G.md) Cautions 4–5, `theorem15_18` (`L4196`) allows zeros of `f` on the boundary circle `sphere 0 r` (where `r / ‖a_i‖ = 1`) and proves circle/interval integrability of `θ ↦ Real.log ‖f(r e^{iθ})‖` explicitly; `theorem15_19` (`L6178`) bridges `Real.log 0 = 0` via `eLogNorm : ℂ → EReal` (`eLogNorm 0 = ⊥`) so `((μ_r : ℝ) : EReal) → eLogNorm (f 0)` holds whether `f 0 ≠ 0` or `f 0 = 0`; and `theorem15_20` (`L7088`) states both `1 - exp(z^k)` and its origin-zero-removed normalization `z ↦ (exp(z^k) - 1) / z^k` (`g 0 = 1`).
5. **Both real `C([0,1], ℝ)` and complex `C([0,1], ℂ)` Müntz–Szász theorems (`15.25`–`15.27`):** To satisfy both [SCOPING-G.md](SCOPING-G.md) (`15.26` over complex `C([0,1])`) and the real-interval / real-exponent formulation `C([0,1], ℝ)`, `Chapter15.lean` proves both the complex theorems (`theorem15_25`, `theorem15_26`, `theorem15_27`) and the real `C(UnitInterval01, ℝ)` theorems (`theorem15_25_real`, `theorem15_26_real`, `theorem15_27_real`).
