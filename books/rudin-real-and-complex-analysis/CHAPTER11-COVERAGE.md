## Representation and fidelity disclosures

1. **Fidelity stop `E-11.5` — printed `11.5(4)` at radius `r = 0` (printed p. 233):** Printed equation `11.5(4)` allows `0 ≤ r < 1` while asserting strict angular decrease `P_r(t) < P_r(δ)` for `0 < δ < |t| ≤ π`. At `r = 0`, `P₀(t) = 1` identically (`poissonKernel_zero`), falsifying the strict inequality (`node11_5_strict_inequality_counterexample` at `L396`, `δ = 1, t = 2`). We preserve the counterexample and the repaired strict inequality under `0 < r` (`node11_5_strict_inequality_corrected` at `L405`), prove non-strict monotonicity `P_r(t) ≤ P_r(δ)` across the full printed `0 ≤ r < 1` range (`node11_5_monotone_non_strict` at `L683`), and prove all remaining identities and bounds of `11.5` (`node11_5_herglotz_re`, `node11_5_hasSum_fourier`, `node11_5_pos`, `node11_5_even`, `node11_5_periodic`, `node11_5_bounds`, `node11_5_integral_eq_one`, `node11_5_tail_tendsto_zero`, packaged in `theorem11_5` at `L729`).
2. **Weak harmonicity definition and reverse regularity equivalence (`11.3`, `11.9`, `11.10`):** `HarmonicOn u Ω` (`L190`) retains Rudin's weak `11.3` definition (`ContinuousOn u Ω`, pointwise existence of the two pure directional second partials `∂²u/∂x²` and `∂²u/∂y²`, and `∂²u/∂x² + ∂²u/∂y² = 0`) without assuming `C²` regularity. In `11.9`–`11.10`, we prove the weak-harmonic maximum principle directly from second directional derivatives (`second_deriv_nonpos_of_isLocalMax`, `weak_harmonic_max_principle_closedBall`, `weak_harmonic_eqOn_closedBall_of_eqOn_sphere`), establish Poisson representation for weakly harmonic functions (`theorem11_9`), and deduce the full reverse regularity equivalence `HarmonicOn u Ω ↔ InnerProductSpace.HarmonicOnNhd u Ω` (`harmonicOn_iff_harmonicOnNhd_real`, `harmonicOn_iff_harmonicOnNhd_complex` at `L1775–L1823`), together with real-analyticity and `C^∞` smoothness (`HarmonicOn.analyticOnNhd_real`, `HarmonicOn.contDiffOn_real`, `HarmonicOn.analyticOnNhd_complex`, `HarmonicOn.contDiffOn_complex`), so every subsequent theorem (`11.10`–`11.14`) applies unconditionally to Rudin's weak `HarmonicOn` predicate.
3. **Uniform equicontinuity in `11.27`–`11.28`:** Rudin's global `ε`-`δ` equicontinuity definition on p. 245 (`|f(x) - f(y)| < ε` whenever `d(x, y) < δ` for all `x, y ∈ X` and `f ∈ 𝓕`) is formalized as `BookEquicontinuous := UniformEquicontinuous` (`L3246`), never weakened to pointwise `Equicontinuous`.
