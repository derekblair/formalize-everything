2. **Stereographic-circle characterization in `14.3`:** Although Rudin states the correspondence between generalized circles on `S²` (`α z conj z + β z + conj β conj z + γ = 0`, `‖β‖² > α γ`) and Euclidean plane intersections with the unit sphere in `ℝ³` without proof in print ([SCOPING-F.md](SCOPING-F.md), `14.3` & `BRIEF.md` §1.2), `theorem14_3_stereographic_circle_iff` (`L971`) proves this characterization in full alongside elementary generation, generalized-circle preservation, unique 3-point interpolation, disc/half-plane conformal equivalence, and the Cayley map (`theorem14_3` at `L1847`).
