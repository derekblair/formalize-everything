## Representation and fidelity disclosures

1. **Oriented-angle preservation (`14.1`–`14.2`):** Because Mathlib's `ConformalAt` deliberately includes orientation-reversing antiholomorphic maps such as `conj` ([SCOPING-F.md](SCOPING-F.md), p. 16), `PreservesAnglesAt` (`L78`) encodes Rudin's exact ray-limit definition via `unitDir z = z / ‖z‖` (`lim_{r → 0⁺} exp(-i θ) A(f(z₀ + r e^{i θ}) - f(z₀)) = e^{i θ₀}` independent of `θ`, on a deleted disc where `f z ≠ f z₀`), and `theorem14_1` (`L595`) explicitly verifies that `conj` does not preserve oriented angles.
2. **Stereographic-circle characterization in `14.3`:** Although Rudin states the correspondence between generalized circles on `S²` (`α z conj z + β z + conj β conj z + γ = 0`, `‖β‖² > α γ`) and Euclidean plane intersections with the unit sphere in `ℝ³` without proof in print ([SCOPING-F.md](SCOPING-F.md), `14.3` & `BRIEF.md` §1.2), `theorem14_3_stereographic_circle_iff` (`L966`) proves this characterization in full alongside elementary generation, generalized-circle preservation, unique 3-point interpolation, disc/half-plane conformal equivalence, and the Cayley map (`theorem14_3` at `L1842`).
3. **Explicit symmetric neighborhood in circular Schwarz reflection (`14.4`):** Per [SCOPING-F.md](SCOPING-F.md) Caution 2, Rudin's circular reflection discussion is geometrically informal; `theorem14_4` (`L2055`) explicates an open neighborhood `W ⊆ {z : ℂ | z ≠ 0}` symmetric under `z ↦ (conj z)⁻¹` (`∀ z ∈ W, (conj z)⁻¹ ∈ W`) partitioning `W` into `W ∩ ball 0 1 ⊆ Ω`, `W ∩ sphere 0 1 = L`, and `{z ∈ W | 1 < ‖z‖}`, proving holomorphy of `circularSchwarzReflection f` (`L2043`) throughout `Ω ∪ W` by Cayley transport to `Chapter11.theorem11_14`.
4. **Full Riemann mapping (`14.8`) and fixed-base-point extremal uniqueness (`14.9`):** Rather than wrapping Mathlib's incomplete `exists_mapsTo_unitBall_injOn_deriv_ne_zero` (which gives injection *into* `ball 0 1` without surjectivity), `theorem14_8` (`L2520`) and `theorem14_9` (`L2570`) prove full bijectivity onto `ball 0 1` (`BijOn h Ω (ball 0 1)`) for every proper simply connected region `Ω ⊊ ℂ`, contrast `ℂ` (`planeHomeomorphUnitBall` vs Liouville `¬ ConformallyEquivalent Set.univ (ball 0 1)`), and establish the extremal bound `‖deriv f z₀‖ ≤ ‖deriv h z₀‖` + rotation characterization `f = λ • h` (`‖λ‖ = 1`) at the fixed normalization point `z₀ ∈ Ω` for all (possibly noninjective) `f ∈ H(Ω)` mapping `Ω` into `ball 0 1`.
5. **Meromorphic punctured-disc convention and guarded summability (`14.13`, `14.15`):** Per [SCOPING-F.md](SCOPING-F.md) Caution 1, `theorem14_13` (`L3460`) and `theorem14_15` (`L4878`) encode holomorphy and injectivity explicitly on the punctured unit disc `ball 0 1 \ {0}`, assert the expansion via `HasSum (fun n : ℕ => a n * z ^ n) (F z - z⁻¹)` for `z ∈ ball 0 1 \ {0}`, verify the pole directly on `RiemannSphere` via `Tendsto (fun z : ℂ => (F z : RiemannSphere)) (𝓝[≠] 0) (𝓝 (∞ : RiemannSphere))`, and prove `Summable (fun n : ℕ => ((n + 1 : ℝ)) * ‖a (n + 1)‖ ^ 2)` before concluding `∑' n : ℕ, ((n + 1 : ℝ)) * ‖a (n + 1)‖ ^ 2 ≤ 1`.
6. **Whole-sequence curve interpolation (`14.16`–`14.17`):** `IsSimpleBoundaryPoint` (`L5104`) requires interpolating the **entire** sequence `α : ℕ → ℂ` (`γ (t n) = α n` for strictly monotone `t : ℕ → ℝ` in `(0, 1)` tending to `1`), without passing to subsequences.
7. **Honest frontier (`14.18`–`14.20` pending, excluded from `Done`):** Carathéodory boundary extension at simple boundary points (`14.18`), homeomorphic extension to `closure Ω → closure U` (`14.19`), and the Jordan-curve remarks (`14.20`, including the unproved Jordan-boundary converse noted in [SCOPING-F.md](SCOPING-F.md) Caution 3) are not formalized in this pass and are excluded from `Done` (`19/22` Chapter 14 nodes counted in `Done`).
