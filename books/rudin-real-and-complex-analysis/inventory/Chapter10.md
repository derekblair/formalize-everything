## Search scope, dependencies, and fidelity record

The negative claims above were checked in `Mathlib/Analysis/Complex`,
`Mathlib/Analysis/Analytic`, `Mathlib/Analysis/Meromorphic`,
`Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Integral/CurveIntegral`, and the relevant topology and
Fourier directories. Full-tree searches included `windingNumber`,
`winding_number`, `Winding`, `contourIntegral`, `pathIntegral`, and the broader
synonyms `curveIntegral`, `lineIntegral`, residue, Rouché, argument principle,
and Casorati. The broader search is important: **Mathlib does have general
curve integrals**, under `curveIntegral`; claiming that all path integration
is absent would be incorrect. The substantial unresolved interface is the
combination of Rudin's finite piecewise `C¹` paths, integral-defined integer
index, equivalent chains/cycles, and global theorems stated using that index.

The mathematical integration inputs for 10.7–10.15, 10.22, 10.35 and 10.44
come from Mathlib's vector/Bochner/interval integration, dominated convergence,
and Fourier/Parseval theory. They may replace Rudin's route through Chapters
1–9; this lane does not require those chapter modules as dependencies.

Fidelity event **E-10.37**: printed p. 221 has a variable-name mismatch between
the displayed index assertion and the final side-membership hypothesis. The
scan was checked visually; this is not an OCR-only artifact. The intended
`z ∈ D₊` reading is an **explicit proposed correction**, not silently accepted
as the literal text. No counterexample or corrected-form proof is claimed
by the inventory. The implementation must keep the node queued until that
event is handled under the brief's fidelity rule. The closure bars on
`D₊` and `D₋` in the side-label assumptions are present in print and are not
errors.

No other false printed assertion is claimed here. In particular the bars in
10.5 and 10.24 are mathematically essential and were confirmed in the scan.
