## Representation and fidelity disclosures

1. **Regular/singular boundary points, natural boundaries, and gap series (`16.1`–`16.7`):** `IsRegularBoundaryPoint` (`L52`) and `IsSingularBoundaryPoint` (`L60`) formalize direct holomorphic continuation across `ζ ∈ sphere a R` on an open disc `ball ζ δ`. `HasRadiusOfConvergenceOne` (`L123`) is bridged both to `closedBall 0 r` uniform summability (`< 1`) and failure on larger balls (`> 1`). `theorem16_4` (`L2423`) proves both the general region non-extendability claim (via `Chapter15.theorem15_11` on boundary-accumulating discrete sets selected from `Chapter13.theorem13_3`) and the dyadic lacunary series `∑_{n≥0} z^{2^n}`. `theorem16_5` (`L1747`) includes Rudin's post-proof uniform-convergence note on `ball ζ ρ` around every regular boundary point.
2. **Morera gluing across lines and circles (`16.8`) and curve continuation / Monodromy (`16.9`–`16.16`):** Rather than restricting to countable exceptional sets, `theorem16_8` (`L2830`) proves Morera holomorphy across any straight line or circle cutting a region `Ω` into disjoint open pieces `Ω₁, Ω₂` via strip-collar approximation of triangle boundary integrals (`Chapter10.theorem10_13`) and Möbius reduction to lines. `FunctionElement` (`L2838`), `DirectContinuation`, `DiscChain`, `ContinuedAlongCurve`, and `IsEndpointFixedCurveFamily` follow Rudin's exact `[0, 1]` and `[0, 1]²` definitions; `theorem16_15` (`L4298`) proves Monodromy for `Chapter10.BookSimplyConnected` plane regions, and `theorem16_16` (`L4667`) proves universal-monodromy failure on every nonsimply connected plane region via local holomorphic logarithms around a bounded-component witness `w₀ ∈ Ωᶜ` from `Chapter13.theorem13_11`.
3. **Modular group, parity subgroup `Γ`, mixed-boundary fundamental set `Q`, and modular function `λ` (`16.17`–`16.20`):** `FundamentalSetQ` (`L5426`) encodes Rudin's exact mixed strict/non-strict inequalities `{z | z.im > 0 ∧ -1 ≤ z.re ∧ z.re < 1 ∧ 1 ≤ ‖2 * z + 1‖ ∧ 1 < ‖2 * z - 1‖}`, proved in `theorem16_19` (`L6268`) to tile `Π⁺` bijectively under `GammaSubgroup = ParityMoebiusSubgroup`. `modularLambda` (`L9468`) and `theorem16_20` (`L11051`) construct `λ ∈ H(Π⁺)` from scratch via the Riemann mapping (`Chapter14.theorem14_8`) of the open hyperbolic triangle `OpenTriangleQ0 = {z | z.im > 0 ∧ 0 < z.re ∧ z.re < 1 ∧ 1 < ‖2 * z - 1‖}`, anti-holomorphic reflection, Carathéodory extension along the hyperbolic geodesic boundary arcs (by Möbius reduction to `Chapter14.theorem14_4` circular Schwarz reflection), cusp removability in the `q = exp(π i z)` chart, and `Γ`-orbit gluing across `Q`, establishing `deriv modularLambda z ≠ 0` on `Π⁺`, `Γ`-invariance, `Set.BijOn modularLambda FundamentalSetQ (ℂ \ {0, 1})`, `modularLambda '' (ball p r ∩ Π⁺) = ℂ \ {0, 1}` for every `p ∈ ℝ` and `r > 0`, and natural boundary `ℝ`.
