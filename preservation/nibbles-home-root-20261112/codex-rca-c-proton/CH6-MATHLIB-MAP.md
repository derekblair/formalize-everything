# Chapter 6 later-node Mathlib claims check

Checked 2026-10-29 against local source at the required Mathlib commit `d13f23b723b8a846827a245b89c10fc7d3f11612`. Printed-node reference: the third-edition source `rca-third-edition.txt`, chapter 6 (printed pp. 121–132). Statements below are paraphrases, not substitutes for the printed claims.

| Node | Printed obligation | Pinned API / actual gap |
|---|---|---|
| 6.9 | A measurable integrable real function strictly between zero and one **everywhere**, for arbitrary sigma-finite positive measure | `exists_pos_lintegral_lt_of_sigmaFinite` (`Integral/Lebesgue/Countable.lean:227`) gives positive NNReal function with finite integral. Clamp to `min g (1/2)`; prove real integrability and strict inequalities. No extra finite-measure assumption needed. |
| 6.10(a) | Unique complex Lebesgue decomposition into absolutely continuous and singular measures; positive finite input gives positive finite components | `ComplexMeasure.singularPart_add_withDensity_rnDeriv_eq` (`VectorMeasure/Decomposition/Lebesgue.lean:440`) has the algebraic equality. Construct its missing sigma-finite instance from the real/imag instances. Complex singularity/uniqueness and positivity preservation need additional proofs; do **not** assume them in the statement. Positive-measure Lebesgue decomposition is available separately. |
| 6.10(b) | Integrable complex density, unique modulo null sets, for the absolutely continuous component | `ComplexMeasure.integrable_rnDeriv`; `SignedMeasure.withDensityᵥ_rnDeriv_eq` (`VectorMeasure/Decomposition/RadonNikodym.lean:29`) on real/imag parts. `Integrable.ae_eq_of_withDensityᵥ_eq` (`VectorMeasure/WithDensity.lean:155`) proves density uniqueness. |
| 6.11 | Null-set absolute continuity iff uniform epsilon–delta control of complex values; positive reference measure need **not** be sigma-finite | No direct complex theorem found. Finite variation plus standard continuity-of-measure argument proves it. An RN proof assuming sigma-finiteness would silently weaken this node. |
| 6.12 | Measurable polar density with modulus one **everywhere**, representing any complex measure relative to its variation | Derive complex RN with respect to finite variation. `Measure.variation_withDensityᵥ` gives variation density norm; positive-density uniqueness then gives modulus one a.e. Replace on the measurable exceptional set to obtain everywhere modulus one. An a.e.-only conclusion is incomplete. |
| 6.13 | Variation of the measure with integrable complex density is the measure with density its modulus | Direct general theorem `Measure.variation_withDensityᵥ` (`VectorMeasure/WithDensityVec.lean:366`). No sigma-finite assumption. |
| 6.14 | Hahn partition with identities for **the canonical Jordan positive and negative parts**, plus their minimality | `SignedMeasure.toJordanDecomposition_spec`, `toMeasureOfZeroLE_real_apply`, `toMeasureOfLEZero_real_apply` give the partition and identities. `Measure.toJordanDecomposition_toSignedMeasure_sub` (`VectorMeasure/Decomposition/JordanSub.lean`) gives minimality via measure subtraction. Merely nonnegative/nonpositive restrictions do not state all printed clauses. |
| 6.15 | Integral pairing defines bounded complex functional on Lp with norm at most Lq norm | `ContinuousLinearMap.lpPairing`, `lpPairing_eq_integral`, `holderL`, `norm_holderL_le` (`Function/Holder.lean:132–155`). Specialize bilinear map to complex multiplication. |
| 6.16 | Every bounded complex functional on Lp, 1 ≤ p < infinity over sigma-finite measure, has a unique Lq density and equal norm | **Substantial gap.** Full-source search for Lp duality, StrongDual/Lp, continuous linear map/Lp, and pairing surjectivity found only the Holder pairing construction. Need the density-extraction, Lq bound, uniqueness, and norm-equality proofs; a pairing construction alone proves only 6.15. |
| 6.17 | Remark tracing p=2 Hilbert representation and proof dependencies | `Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Dual.lean` provides Hilbert representation. This is an explanatory numbered node, not a second general Lp-duality theorem. |
| 6.18 | Complex integration via polar density, additivity, bounded functional on C0; Cc dense and unique bounded extension | Vector integration and C0 normed complete-space infrastructure exist; `VectorMeasure/Integral.lean`, `SetIntegral.lean`, `WithDensityVec.lean`, `Topology/ContinuousMap/ZeroAtInfty.lean`. Requires identifying that integral with the printed polar-density convention and proving the stated functional properties. |
| 6.19 | Every bounded complex functional on C0 of **any locally compact Hausdorff space** has a unique regular complex Borel representing measure, with norm equal to total variation | **Substantial gap.** `Integral/RieszMarkovKakutani/Real.lean` covers **positive real** functionals on Cc; NNReal version also only positive. Entire Mathlib source contains no complex arbitrary-functional RMK theorem. Need decomposition/domination of functionals, construction of complex measure, regularity, extension to C0, uniqueness, and exact norm. Restricting to compact/second-countable X or positive real functionals would not discharge this node. |
